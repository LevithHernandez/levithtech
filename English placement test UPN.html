Text 1

Emma works in a small bookstore in her town. She starts work at nine o'clock and usually finishes at five. She enjoys helping customers find interesting books. On Saturdays, the store is very busy, so Emma often has lunch later than usual.

Text 2

Many cities are encouraging residents to use bicycles instead of cars. Cycling can reduce traffic congestion and air pollution while also improving physical health. However, people may avoid cycling when roads are unsafe or when there are no secure places to leave their bicycles. For this reason, some local governments are investing in protected bike lanes and public bicycle parking.

Text 3

Remote work has transformed the way many organizations operate. Employees often value the flexibility it offers, but working from home can also blur the boundary between professional and personal life. Research suggests that remote workers are more productive when they have clear routines, defined working hours, and a dedicated workspace. Organizations also need effective communication practices to prevent employees from feeling isolated or disconnected from their teams.
